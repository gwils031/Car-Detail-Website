Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it when you use our website and mobile detailing services.

Effective date: April 6, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you request or book service, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, service address, vehicle/service preferences, booking details, and any notes you provide.

2. How We Use Information

We use your information to schedule and deliver services, communicate confirmations and updates, provide customer support, improve service quality, and maintain business records.

3. Analytics and Site Activity

We may collect limited website analytics such as page visits, booking funnel events, and service selection behavior to improve reliability and user experience. We do not intentionally collect payment card data on this website.

4. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data only with service providers needed to operate the booking workflow, communications, and hosting infrastructure, or when required by law.

5. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to operate the business, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Retention periods vary by record type.

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect information. No internet transmission or storage system is guaranteed to be 100% secure.

7. Your Choices

You can request updates or deletion of your information where applicable by contacting us. We may need to retain certain records for operational or legal reasons.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates are effective when posted on this page with a revised effective date.
